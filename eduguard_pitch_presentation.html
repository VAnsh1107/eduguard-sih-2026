The Institutional Bottleneck

Why Traditional University Systems Miss At-Risk Students

Universities have plenty of data, but it is trapped in silos. By the time a student misses midterm exams or stops attending, it's often too late to save their semester.

⏳
1. Purely Reactive Action
Institutions usually notice dropouts only when exam fees or next-semester registrations fail—long after the student has disengaged.
🗄️
2. Disconnected Data Silos
Attendance registers, LMS login logs, library databases, and counselor notes never speak to each other in real-time.
🔒
3. Black-Box Confusion
Generic alerts don't provide actionable context. Teachers need transparent, prescriptive guidance tailored to each student.
